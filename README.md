## What I do

I turn ambiguous requirements into software that teams can confidently ship and maintain. Today I lead delivery for a six-person engineering team on a large DoD enterprise platform, setting technical direction while owning scope, sequencing, and stakeholder alignment.

My route into engineering leadership was not conventional. I supervised intelligence operations in the Marine Corps and mentored officers in law enforcement before leading software teams. The common thread is simple: understand the mission, make the hard calls visible, and help people do their best work.
